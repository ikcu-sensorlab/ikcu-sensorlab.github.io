research

Sensing technologies, functional materials, biological systems and computational intelligence

Our research integrates advanced sensing technologies, functional materials, biological systems and computational intelligence to address challenges in biomedical engineering.

Electrochemical Biosensors and Bioelectronics

We develop sensitive, selective and cost-effective electrochemical platforms for detecting biologically relevant molecules, from microelectrodes and nanoelectrodes to screen-printed electrodes and custom electrochemical instrumentation.

MicroelectrodesScreen-printed electrodesInstrumentationNanomaterials

Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Diagnostics

We apply machine learning, deep learning and computer vision to make biomedical measurements more accurate, reliable and automated.

Colorimetric analysisSignal processingSmartphone diagnosticsBiological image analysis

Wearable and Point-of-Care Technologies

We build portable, wearable and minimally invasive sensing platforms for personalized health monitoring.

Flexible electronicsPaper-based devicesSmartphone appsIntegrated systems

Functional Nanomaterials and Printed Sensors

We work with carbon nanotubes, carbon fibers, nanostructured metal oxides and conductive inks to fabricate high-performance sensors, with an emphasis on scalable, affordable and flexible manufacturing.

Carbon nanotubesCarbon fibersMetal oxidesConductive inks

Micro/Nanobiosensors and Single-Cell Analysis

We use microscale and nanoscale electrochemical platforms to study cellular activity, biomolecular secretion and cellular microenvironments.

MicroprobesElectrode arraysRedox cyclingElectrochemical imaging

Photomedicine and Cellular Monitoring

We monitor cellular responses to therapeutic interventions such as photodynamic therapy with electrochemical and optical methods.

Reactive oxygen speciesCell viabilityNeurotoxicityMicrotissues

Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering

We explore functional biomaterials, bioactive interfaces and microengineered platforms for tissue engineering and regenerative medicine.

Neural guidanceNerve regenerationCell differentiation
