projects

Ongoing and completed research projects

Ongoing projects

AI-Assisted Wearable Sensing System for Personalized Sports and Exercise Monitoring

Ongoing  TÜBİTAK 1001 · 2023–Present

A wearable system for minimally invasive colorimetric measurement of critical exercise biomarkers, combining biosensors, wearable platforms and artificial intelligence for personalized physiological monitoring.

Battery-Free Portable Aptasensor Platform for Melatonin Monitoring in Patients with Epilepsy

Ongoing  TÜSEB Group B R&D · 2024–Present

A non-invasive, battery-free and portable aptasensor platform for melatonin monitoring, with blockchain-based technologies for secure biomedical data management.

Completed projects

Cost-Effective Screen-Printed Electrodes Using Novel Nanocarbon-Based Conductive Inks

Completed  TÜBİTAK 1005 · 2023–2025

Flexible and rigid screen-printed electrodes made with custom nanocarbon-based conductive inks for affordable, scalable sensor manufacturing.

Bioactive Peptide-Conjugated Nanofibrous Nerve Guidance Channels for Peripheral Nerve Repair

Completed  TÜBİTAK 1001 · 2022–2024

Bifunctional nanofibrous nerve guidance channels with bioactive peptides to support peripheral nerve regeneration.

Integration of Machine Learning with Paper-Based Colorimetric Sensor Technologies

Completed  University BAP · 2019–2020

Machine learning classifiers combined with paper-based analytical devices for quantitative determination of analytes.

Development of Biochips for Neural Network Formation

Completed  TÜBİTAK 3501 · 2016–2020

Microengineered biochips with carbon nanotube structures to investigate and guide neuronal network formation.

Non-Invasive Electrochemical Monitoring of Osteogenic Differentiation

Completed  Research Project · 2017–2020

Time-dependent monitoring of osteogenic differentiation in mesenchymal stem cells using redox cycling-based carbon fiber electrodes.

Carbon Fiber Electrodes for High-Sensitivity ATP Detection

Completed  TÜBİTAK · 2015–2018

Carbon fiber microelectrodes, functionally modified for highly sensitive electrochemical detection of ATP.

Fabrication and Characterization of Ultramicro Multi-Electrode Probes

Completed  University BAP · 2015–2016

Miniaturized probes integrating multiple carbon fiber electrodes for localized electrochemical measurements in biological systems.
