Our research integrates advanced sensing technologies, functional materials, biological systems and computational intelligence to address challenges in biomedical engineering.

<div class="lab-grid">

  <div class="lab-card lab-wide" id="electrochemical-biosensors">

    <p>We develop sensitive, selective and cost-effective electrochemical platforms for detecting biologically relevant molecules, from microelectrodes and nanoelectrodes to screen-printed electrodes and custom electrochemical instrumentation.</p>
    <div class="lab-tags"><span class="lab-tag">Microelectrodes</span><span class="lab-tag">Screen-printed electrodes</span><span class="lab-tag">Instrumentation</span><span class="lab-tag">Nanomaterials</span></div>

    <p>We apply machine learning, deep learning and computer vision to make biomedical measurements more accurate, reliable and automated.</p>
    <div class="lab-tags"><span class="lab-tag">Colorimetric analysis</span><span class="lab-tag">Signal processing</span><span class="lab-tag">Smartphone diagnostics</span><span class="lab-tag">Biological image analysis</span></div>

    <p>We build portable, wearable and minimally invasive sensing platforms for personalized health monitoring.</p>
    <div class="lab-tags"><span class="lab-tag">Flexible electronics</span><span class="lab-tag">Paper-based devices</span><span class="lab-tag">Smartphone apps</span><span class="lab-tag">Integrated systems</span></div>

    <p>We work with carbon nanotubes, carbon fibers, nanostructured metal oxides and conductive inks to fabricate high-performance sensors, with an emphasis on scalable, affordable and flexible manufacturing.</p>
    <div class="lab-tags"><span class="lab-tag">Carbon nanotubes</span><span class="lab-tag">Carbon fibers</span><span class="lab-tag">Metal oxides</span><span class="lab-tag">Conductive inks</span></div>

    <p>We use microscale and nanoscale electrochemical platforms to study cellular activity, biomolecular secretion and cellular microenvironments.</p>
    <div class="lab-tags"><span class="lab-tag">Microprobes</span><span class="lab-tag">Electrode arrays</span><span class="lab-tag">Redox cycling</span><span class="lab-tag">Electrochemical imaging</span></div>
