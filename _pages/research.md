## Electrochemical Biosensors and Bioelectronics

We develop sensitive, selective, and cost-effective electrochemical sensing platforms for the detection of biologically relevant molecules. Our work includes microelectrodes, nanoelectrodes, screen-printed electrodes, electrochemical instrumentation, and functional nanomaterials.

## Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Diagnostics

We investigate machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision approaches to improve the accuracy, reliability, and automation of biomedical measurements. Our research includes AI-assisted colorimetric analysis, electrochemical signal processing, smartphone-based diagnostics, and biological image analysis.

## Wearable and Point-of-Care Technologies

We develop portable, wearable, and minimally invasive sensing platforms for personalized health monitoring. Our research combines flexible electronics, paper-based analytical devices, smartphone applications, and integrated measurement systems.

## Functional Nanomaterials and Printed Sensors

We investigate carbon nanotubes, carbon fibers, nanostructured metal oxides, and functional conductive inks for the fabrication of high-performance sensors. Particular emphasis is placed on scalable manufacturing, affordability, flexibility, and biomedical compatibility.

## Micro/Nanobiosensors and Single-Cell Analysis

Our research explores microscale and nanoscale electrochemical sensing platforms for investigating cellular activity, biomolecular secretion, and cellular microenvironments. We employ electrochemical microprobes, electrode arrays, redox cycling, and advanced electrochemical imaging techniques.

## Photomedicine and Cellular Monitoring

We investigate electrochemical and optical approaches for monitoring cellular responses to therapeutic interventions, including photodynamic therapy. Our research explores reactive oxygen species, cell viability, neurotoxicity, and the assessment of cellular and microtissue responses.

## Biomaterials and Tissue Engineering

We explore functional biomaterials, bioactive interfaces, and microengineered platforms for tissue engineering and regenerative medicine, with applications in neural guidance, peripheral nerve regeneration, and cellular differentiation.
