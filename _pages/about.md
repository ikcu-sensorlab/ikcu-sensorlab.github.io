**Engineering intelligent sensing technologies for the future of healthcare.**

Welcome to the IKCU Sensor Lab at the Department of Biomedical Engineering, İzmir Kâtip Çelebi University, Türkiye.

Led by Prof. Dr. Mustafa Şen, our research group brings together biomedical engineering, electrochemistry, materials science, micro- and nanotechnology, and artificial intelligence to develop innovative sensing technologies for biological and biomedical applications.

Our research focuses on the development of electrochemical and optical biosensors, intelligent diagnostic platforms, wearable sensing systems, and micro- and nanoscale devices for biological analysis. By integrating advanced sensing materials with electronic instrumentation, computational methods, and machine learning, we aim to transform complex analytical processes into accessible, reliable, and cost-effective technologies.

From single-cell analysis to personalized health monitoring, we explore new approaches to understanding biological systems and addressing real-world healthcare challenges.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge fundamental biological research and engineering innovation through the development of intelligent, affordable, and accessible biomedical technologies.

We combine sensor fabrication, experimental validation, hardware development, and artificial intelligence to create integrated analytical systems with potential applications in healthcare, point-of-care diagnostics, personalized medicine, and biological research.

## Our Research

- Electrochemical and optical biosensors
- Artificial intelligence-assisted sensing and diagnostics
- Wearable and portable biomedical devices
- Micro- and nanoscale bioanalytical systems
- Functional materials and printed electronics
- Single-cell analysis and electrochemical imaging
- Tissue engineering and regenerative medicine

## Interdisciplinary Research and Collaboration

We believe that meaningful biomedical innovation emerges at the intersection of different scientific disciplines. Our team brings together researchers with diverse academic backgrounds and technical expertise, fostering a collaborative environment that connects biology, engineering, materials science, and computer science.

We welcome opportunities for national and international research collaborations, interdisciplinary projects, and the development of next-generation biomedical technologies.
