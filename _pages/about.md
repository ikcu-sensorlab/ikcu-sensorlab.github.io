## Our mission

Our mission is to bridge fundamental biological research and engineering innovation by developing intelligent, affordable and accessible biomedical technologies. We combine sensor fabrication, experimental validation, hardware development and artificial intelligence into integrated analytical systems for healthcare, point-of-care diagnostics, personalized medicine and biological research.

## Collaboration

Meaningful biomedical innovation happens where disciplines meet. Our team brings together biology, engineering, materials science and computer science, and we welcome national and international research collaborations and interdisciplinary projects.
