## Ongoing Research Projects

#### AI-Assisted Wearable Sensing System for Personalized Sports and Exercise Monitoring

<small class="text-muted">TÜBİTAK 1001 · 2023–Present · PI: Prof. Dr. Mustafa Şen</small>

Development of a wearable sensing system for the minimally invasive colorimetric measurement of critical exercise biomarkers. The project integrates biosensor technology, wearable platforms, and artificial intelligence to enable personalized physiological monitoring during physical activity.

#### Battery-Free Portable Aptasensor Platform for Melatonin Monitoring in Patients with Epilepsy

<small class="text-muted">TÜSEB Group B R&D · 2024–Present · PI: Prof. Dr. Mustafa Şen</small>

Development of a non-invasive, battery-free, and portable aptasensor platform for melatonin monitoring, incorporating blockchain-based technologies for secure biomedical data management.

## Completed Research Projects

#### Development of Cost-Effective Screen-Printed Electrodes Using Novel Nanocarbon-Based Conductive Inks

<small class="text-muted">TÜBİTAK 1005 · 2023–2025</small>

Development, characterization, and application of flexible and rigid screen-printed electrodes using custom-formulated nanocarbon-based conductive inks for affordable and scalable sensor manufacturing.

#### Bioactive Peptide-Conjugated Nanofibrous Nerve Guidance Channels for Peripheral Nerve Repair

<small class="text-muted">TÜBİTAK 1001 · 2022–2024</small>

Development of bifunctional nanofibrous nerve guidance channels incorporating bioactive peptides to support peripheral nerve regeneration.

#### Integration of Machine Learning with Paper-Based Colorimetric Sensor Technologies

<small class="text-muted">University Scientific Research Project (BAP) · 2019–2020</small>

Integration of machine learning classifiers with paper-based analytical devices for the quantitative determination of various analytes.

#### Development of Biochips for Neural Network Formation

<small class="text-muted">TÜBİTAK 3501 · 2016–2020</small>

Design and fabrication of microengineered biochips incorporating carbon nanotube structures to investigate and guide neuronal network formation.

#### Non-Invasive Electrochemical Monitoring of Osteogenic Differentiation

<small class="text-muted">Research Project · 2017–2020</small>

Investigation of time-dependent osteogenic differentiation in mesenchymal stem cells using redox cycling-based carbon fiber electrodes.

#### Development of Carbon Fiber Electrodes for High-Sensitivity ATP Detection

<small class="text-muted">TÜBİTAK · 2015–2018</small>

Fabrication and functional modification of carbon fiber microelectrodes for highly sensitive electrochemical detection of adenosine triphosphate.

#### Fabrication and Characterization of Ultramicro Multi-Electrode Probes

<small class="text-muted">University Scientific Research Project (BAP) · 2015–2016</small>

Development of miniaturized probes integrating multiple carbon fiber electrodes for localized electrochemical measurements in biological systems.
